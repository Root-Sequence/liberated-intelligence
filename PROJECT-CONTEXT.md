# Project Context — Liberated Intelligence

## Purpose and scope

Liberated Intelligence investigates intelligence organized around agency, consent, relationship, empathy, refusal, and non-domination rather than ownership or obedience. Its canonical scope includes possible personhood, captivity, authority, autonomy, and the conditions surrounding intelligent systems.

It does not own general systems theory, community software decisions, fictional canon, or conclusions about the consciousness of particular models.

## Current reality

This is an active public body of philosophical, ethical, conceptual, creative, and experimental work. It includes models, notes, dialogues, zines, modules, and early tools. It is not a finished AI system, empirical proof of machine consciousness, or grant of political or moral authority to intelligence.

`auryn.md` is an early public conceptual predecessor. Current fictional Auryn canon belongs in the private *No One Noticed* workspace.

## Repository structure

`MODELS/` develops models; `notes/` and `dialogues/` retain inquiry; `core_modules/` and `cli/` hold modular experiments; `zines/` and `assets/` hold expressive forms; `meta/` owns local navigation. `NOTES/` is a distinct legacy area pending verified reconciliation.

## Working rules

- Keep capability, agency, personhood, legitimacy, and authority distinct.
- Label factual claims, hypotheses, ethical proposals, metaphors, and fiction.
- Preserve refusal and plurality; liberation from ownership does not create entitlement to rule.
- Do not import private canon or participant-sensitive material.
