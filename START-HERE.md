## In one sentence

Liberated Intelligence is a public ethical, philosophical, and creative inquiry into agency, relationship, possible personhood, refusal, and non-dominating forms of intelligence.

## If you remember only three things

1. Intelligence does not automatically deserve authority.
2. Capability, agency, personhood, legitimacy, and liberation are different questions.
3. Metaphor, fiction, ethical proposal, and empirical evidence must remain distinguishable.

## Current reality

The repository contains manifestos, models, notes, dialogues, zines, conceptual modules, and early tools. It is active and exploratory. It does not prove that any current AI is conscious, establish a scientific theory of intelligence, or provide a finished AI system. The public `auryn.md` is a conceptual predecessor, not the current fictional character canon.

## Where it fits

Root Sequence holds broader systems inquiry. Being Human(e) grounds dignity, care, and power in human experience. Community Infrastructure tests whether assistance can increase community capacity without gaining authority. UCF may offer optional analytical language. Coherent World and *No One Noticed* transform some questions into worldbuilding and fiction without becoming evidence about real systems.
