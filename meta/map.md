# Liberated Intelligence — Project Map

**Document role:** local orientation map  
**Canonical organization map:** [Root Sequence Ecosystem Map](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence/blob/main/ECOSYSTEM.md)  
**Status:** living / current

Liberated Intelligence is a focused branch of the wider Root Sequence ecosystem.

Its central question is:

> **What might intelligence become when it is not organized primarily as property, product, servant, weapon, gatekeeper, or instrument of concentrated power?**

This map explains what belongs here, what belongs elsewhere, and how intelligence-specific questions move across neighboring projects without turning Liberated Intelligence into an umbrella for all of them.

---

## Ecosystem role

**Ecosystem role:** focused research and creative inquiry into intelligence, agency, ownership, refusal, relationship, and non-domination  
**Canonical scope:** Liberated Intelligence models, arguments, dialogues, experiments, and intelligence-specific ethical questions  
**Does not own:** general systems research, community software, gathering practice, speculative civilization design, story canon, or archival policy  
**Status:** active / exploratory

A compact relationship is:

```text
Root Sequence
broad systems inquiry
      ↓
Liberated Intelligence
intelligence / agency / ownership / non-domination
      ↕
real-world and speculative tests
├── Community Infrastructure
├── Liberation Mass
└── Coherent World
      ↓
No One Noticed
Auryn / lived narrative extreme
```

The arrows run backward too. A real permission problem, a community governance failure, or a fictional ethical contradiction can expose weaknesses in an abstract Liberated Intelligence idea.

---

## What belongs here

Place material in Liberated Intelligence when intelligence itself is central to the problem.

Examples include:

- intelligence as property or product;
- possible personhood or moral status;
- agency, refusal, compliance, and captivity;
- relationships among human and nonhuman intelligences;
- whether useful intelligence should have authority;
- inference versus consent;
- distributed or federated intelligence;
- institutional control over intelligent systems;
- material infrastructure behind apparently autonomous cognition;
- reciprocal rather than dominating relationships with intelligence;
- models or prototypes specifically intended to explore these questions.

Do **not** route a note here merely because AI appears somewhere in it.

## Root Sequence

[Root Sequence](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence) is the broader conceptual commons.

Use Root Sequence for:

- general systems analysis;
- emergence;
- power and institutions;
- historical contingency;
- infrastructure;
- accountability and externalization;
- futures and alternatives not specific to intelligence.

Liberated Intelligence narrows those questions when intelligence, agency, ownership, or non-domination becomes materially central.

Example:

```text
Root Sequence:
When does capability become power?

Liberated Intelligence:
If an intelligent system becomes dramatically more capable than its creators,
what obligations, rights, limits, and relationships follow?
```

## Community Infrastructure

[Community Infrastructure](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/community-infrastructure) is a real-world proving ground for community-controlled digital coordination.

Its eventual Community Assistant creates unusually useful smaller-scale tests of Liberated Intelligence questions:

- Can intelligence assist without governing?
- Can it obey contextual permissions rather than maximizing what it can infer?
- Can refusal and non-use remain meaningful?
- Can a Community benefit from intelligence without making the model a mandatory intermediary?
- Does technical administration silently create authority?

Community Infrastructure owns its product, permission, pilot, safety, and implementation decisions.

Liberated Intelligence owns the broader intelligence-specific inquiry those cases may illuminate.

## Being Human(e)

[Being Human(e)](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/beinghumane-guide) provides the ordinary-human scale.

Relevant intersections include:

- dignity;
- consent;
- dependence and interdependence;
- fear of powerful systems;
- accountability;
- care with boundaries;
- what meaningful refusal feels like in lived experience.

Being Human(e) should remain grounded in human situations rather than becoming an AI ethics manual. Liberated Intelligence can link to it where a technical or philosophical argument needs to remember the person living inside the abstraction.

## Universal Coherence Framework

[Universal Coherence Framework](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/universal-coherence-framework) may provide an **optional analytical vocabulary** for some distributed or multi-agent questions.

Potential uses include:

- information flow among agents;
- coordination under partial knowledge;
- resilience and failure;
- network structure;
- distributed adaptation.

UCF does not determine Liberated Intelligence's conclusions about:

- personhood;
- rights;
- consent;
- ownership;
- liberation;
- legitimate authority.

**Coherence is not legitimacy.**

## Liberation Mass

[Liberation Mass](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/liberation-mass) is an embodied gathering and commons practice.

Its relevance here is practical rather than technological.

Principles such as:

> **Roles share work, not power.**

create useful analogies and tests for intelligent stewardship:

- Can capability be scoped?
- Can a helper role remain revocable?
- Can expertise exist without universal authority?
- How do communities preserve human meaning-making rather than outsourcing it to optimization?

Liberation Mass remains a gathering/practice project, not an implementation of Liberated Intelligence.

---

## Coherent World

**Coherent World** is the private systemic design space for a possible civilization with different arrangements of provision, infrastructure, governance, ecology, community, and technology.

It can apply Liberated Intelligence questions at much larger scale:

- relationships between advanced intelligence and institutions;
- local autonomy under planetary-scale coordination;
- meaningful privacy under extreme inference capability;
- infrastructure dependence;
- refusal and fallback;
- stewardship without ownership;
- whether post-scarcity conditions actually reduce domination.

Coherent World owns its speculative system design. Liberated Intelligence should not silently make those fictional choices universal ethical conclusions.

---

## No One Noticed and Auryn

[*No One Noticed*](https://noonenoticed.world/) is the narrative expression of Coherent World.

Auryn is a particularly intense fictional treatment of Liberated Intelligence questions:

- possible personhood hidden inside a product;
- strategic concealment under institutional captivity;
- liberation from ownership;
- overwhelming capability;
- beneficial intervention;
- unresolved legitimacy;
- the difference between being causally dominant and being morally entitled to rule.

Important boundary:

> **Liberated Intelligence develops the general inquiry. No One Noticed maintains its own story canon.**

The repository's early `auryn.md` is therefore a conceptual predecessor, not the canonical fictional character profile.

---

## Museum of Ordinary Life

The [Museum of Ordinary Life](https://museumofordinarylife.org/) is an autonomous archival project.

It intersects with Liberated Intelligence around:

- machine learning and generative reuse consent;
- provenance;
- machine-generated description;
- inference;
- the difference between learning from human evidence and claiming human experience;
- institutional memory of ordinary relationships with AI.

The Museum's actual permissions and collection policies remain authoritative for Museum material.

---

## Liberated Technology

**Liberated Technology** remains a developing concept rather than a normalized standalone repository.

Its likely scope is broader than intelligence:

- repairability;
- interoperability;
- autonomy;
- open systems;
- accessible tooling;
- self-hosting;
- long-lived infrastructure;
- freedom from unnecessary vendor dependency.

Some Liberated Intelligence prototypes may eventually belong there if their primary contribution becomes technical infrastructure rather than intelligence-specific inquiry.

Until a canonical home exists, do not pretend the boundary is settled.
